Lingo bundles or derives from the following. Thank you to everyone involved.

mGBA
  Copyright (c) 2013-2026 Jeffrey Pfau and contributors.
  Mozilla Public License 2.0. https://mgba.io  Source: https://github.com/mgba-emu/mgba
  Lingo's changes to mGBA are in patches/ in the Lingo repository and are also MPL 2.0.

JMdict (lingo.dic)
  Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG), Creative Commons
  Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0. https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html
  Packaged for Python as jamdict-data. lingo.dic is a converted, shortened copy and
  is shared under the same licence.

KANJIDIC (kanji meanings and readings in Kaku)
  EDRDG, Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0.

KanjiVG (stroke order and shapes in Kaku)
  Copyright (C) 2009-2011 Ulrich Apel. Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0.
  https://kanjivg.tagaini.net

Klee One (klee.ttf)
  Copyright 2020 The Klee Project Authors. SIL Open Font License 1.1.
  https://github.com/fontworks-fonts/Klee

M PLUS Rounded 1c (ui.ttf)
  Copyright 2016 The M+ Project Authors. SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Reference glyphs for screen reading (glyphs.bin)
  Rendered from DotGothic16 (Fontworks), M PLUS Rounded 1c and M PLUS 1 Code (The M+
  Project Authors) and BIZ UDGothic (Morisawa), all SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Stroke matching
  Follows the approach of Hanzi Writer by David Chanin (MIT licence).

Emulator cores (cores/*.self), each linked with Lingo's front end
  Built unmodified from the libretro project's repositories at the commits listed in
  retro/cores.txt in the Lingo repository; that file and the build scripts are the
  corresponding source. Each keeps its own licence:
  FCEUmm (GPL 2), Snes9x 2005 (Snes9x licence, non-commercial), Genesis Plus GX
  (non-commercial licence), Beetle PCE Fast / WonderSwan / NeoPop / VB (GPL 2),
  PCSX ReARMed (GPL 2), fMSX (Marat Fayzullin's licence, non-commercial),
  FinalBurn Neo (non-commercial licence), PokeMini (GPL 3), Yabause (GPL 2).
  Lingo itself is free and must stay free to include them.
  libretro.h and libretro-common: MIT, the libretro team.

whisper.cpp (speech recognition) and the Whisper "tiny" model (lingo/whisper.bin)
  whisper.cpp and ggml: Copyright (c) 2023-2024 Georgi Gerganov and contributors, MIT.
  Whisper model weights: Copyright (c) 2022 OpenAI, MIT.

Text tables and statistics
  Pokémon Game Boy character layouts follow the pret disassembly projects
  (github.com/pret/pokered, pokecrystal). Kana pair statistics are computed from
  JMdict (above) and Kaku's lessons.

Networking
  curl (curl licence), OpenSSL (Apache 2.0), Mozilla's root certificates via the curl
  project (cacert.pem, MPL 2.0), QR codes by Project Nayuki's QR Code generator (MIT).

Lingo and Kaku
  By Troy Stokoe.
